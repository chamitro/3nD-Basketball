3nD Basketball

A cartoon 3-on-3 basketball manager, played with friends. Talk to your players, set the plan, and the match plays itself three times a week.

Put it on your home screen

It is a web app: install it once and it opens like any other app, with its own icon. Your team lives inside it, so always play from that icon.

  1. Android Chrome
    Open the game, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  2. iPhone and iPad Safari
    Open the game, tap the share button ⬆︎, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Laptop Chrome · Edge
    Open the game and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or the browser menu, then Install 3nD Basketball.

How it goes

Draft seven players and name your team. Before each match, talk to your players, set the line-up, the playing time, the dials and the half-time orders, and vote for the rule of the next match. Matches are played on Sunday, Wednesday and Friday at 18:00 UTC. Then come back for the score, the box score and what the players think of you.
